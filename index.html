Priya Raghunathan

Scientific Director of the Center for Riverine Systems at the Marlowe Institute for Population Health.

Profile

Priya Raghunathan leads the Center for Riverine Systems at the Marlowe Institute for Population Health. Her work connects hydrological science with the governance of shared water resources.

Education

  • DPhil in Environmental Hydrology from the University of Wexford
  • MSc in Public Health Data Science from Kestrel Bay University

Research interests

Research interests include river basin modelling, contaminant transport and community water governance.

Previous appointments

  • Senior Lecturer in Environmental Systems at Northgate College (2018-2021)
  • Postdoctoral Researcher at the Helmholtz Centre for Aquatic Research (2015-2018)

Notable publications

  • Seasonal nitrate pulses in a lowland agricultural catchment — Water Research (2024), first author
  • Contaminant transport modelling for community water governance — Journal of Hydrology (2023), first author
  • A low-cost sensor network for river turbidity monitoring — Environmental Monitoring and Assessment (2022), co-author
  • Climate-driven shifts in dissolved organic carbon export — Biogeochemistry (2021), first author
  • Participatory water quality monitoring in peri-urban settlements — Water International (2020), co-author
  • Uncertainty in phosphate load estimates from sparse sampling — Hydrological Processes (2019), first author

Honours

  • 2024 Riverine Science Prize from the Wexford Water Authority
  • Invited keynote at the 2025 Global Rivers Symposium
